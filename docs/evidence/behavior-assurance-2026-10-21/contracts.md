## Why these families were selected

Repeated reports concentrate on mismatched boundaries, weak oracles and
composed state transitions. They do not establish that those areas have a
higher production defect rate: review effort and changed code were not held
constant. The history tells us which counterexamples to try; current source
and observed assertion behavior decide whether new work has detection value.

The current research found two inexpensive, concrete regression-oracle
improvements. It also rejected several plausible additions after discovering
existing assertions or narrower historical evidence. Additional universal
proofs, arbitrary platform cells and duplicate helper tests did not meet the
same evidence standard.
